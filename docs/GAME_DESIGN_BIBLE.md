# ZERO GRACE: TRINITY — Game Design Bible

## Identity
Stylish social/psychological party RPG built around three interlocking dimensions: daily/social life, internal/otherworld exploration and strategic combat. Character relationships and personal choices must materially connect to gameplay.

## Core loop
Calendar/time block → relationships/school/work/city activity → investigate anomaly → dungeon/otherworld exploration → party battle → recover truth/resource → relationship/character consequence → schedule next priorities.

## Trinity system
Three linked progression axes—SELF, BOND, CONFLICT (working labels)—feed abilities, dialogue options, party synergies and story outcomes. Final naming/content remains canon decision.

## Combat
Turn-based/strategic party combat with weakness/status/synergy, team follow-ups and relationship-derived abilities. Turn-Based RPG Template is accelerator; rules remain canonical/data-driven.

## Social simulation
Calendar, availability, locations, relationship states, confidant-like arcs without copying protected expression, activities, stats and consequence. Narrative Pro 2 owns canonical Unreal quest/dialogue/state backbone.

## Dungeons/world
Stylized spaces externalize psychological/social conflicts; handcrafted structure with systemic encounters and puzzles.

## V1→V3
V1 proves one calendar chapter, city hub, relationship arcs, dungeon and complete combat loop. V2 expands cast/locations/dungeons/social systems. V3 completes campaign, alternate outcomes/postgame and AXIOM-native build.
